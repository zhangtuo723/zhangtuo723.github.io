The GLB is retained unmodified. `factory-robot.js` clones its materials at runtime and changes the orange shell to cool silver and the grey trim to titanium blue-grey. The authored skinning, facial morph targets and animation clips remain intact. This is an illustrated robot, not a claim of a deployed AI control system.

The separate Three.js r170 `GLTFLoader.js` runtime is provided under the existing Three.js MIT licence in `assets/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
